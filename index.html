About the Player

Lionel Messi is widely regarded as one of the greatest football players of all time. He has won multiple Ballon d'Or awards and holds numerous records.

Born in Rosario, Argentina, Messi joined Barcelona's youth academy at age 13. He spent over 20 years at the club, becoming their all-time top scorer.

In 2023, Messi moved to Inter Miami CF in Major League Soccer, bringing his incredible talent to American football.
